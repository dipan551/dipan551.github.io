Amul — Google Search Ads Concept

Self-directed case study · Not an official Amul campaign

Featured project

Brief I set myself

Amul relies mostly on brand recall built through decades of print, TV and its famous topical ads. I wanted to explore: could Search ads capture the "where do I buy this" and "which Amul product" moments that brand advertising doesn't cover?

Objective & audience

Goal: drive traffic to Amul's online store and locate-a-parlour page.
Audience: urban, 18–40, health- and convenience-driven shoppers already searching for dairy products online.

Keyword clusters

  • Brand: amul milk, amul ice cream, amul butter online
  • Product intent: amul high protein milk, amul buttermilk price, amul paneer online
  • Local intent: amul parlour near me, buy amul milk online Delhi

Sample ad copy (written for practice)

Ad · Search
Amul High Protein Milk | Order Online
www.amul.com/protein-milk
27g protein per litre. Fresh, homegrown, delivered. Find your nearest Amul parlour or order online today.
Ad · Search
Buy Amul Products Online — Amul.com
www.amul.com/store
Milk, butter, cheese & ice cream from India's most trusted dairy brand. Same-day delivery in select cities.

Structure & bidding

3 ad groups by product line (Milk, Butter & Cheese, Ice Cream), tightly matched keywords per group, Maximize Conversions bidding once enough conversion data exists — Manual CPC to start.

How I approach a campaign.

The workflow I follow, even for practice projects.

01

Research

Understand the business, its customers and what they're actually searching for.

02

Structure

Build tightly themed ad groups with matching keywords and ad copy.

03

Launch

Set a sensible budget and bidding strategy, then monitor closely in the first days.

04

Analyze

05

Report
